NASA-STD-8719.14 and ESA debris guidance emphasize mission planning practices that reduce
fragmentation risk and avoid unnecessary maneuvers. Operators should preserve sufficient
delta-v margin for collision avoidance and controlled end-of-life operations.

When fuel margin becomes constrained, planners should reprioritize high-risk targets and
avoid trajectory decisions that create dense conjunction windows. Documentation and review
of fuel-budget assumptions are required before operational execution.
